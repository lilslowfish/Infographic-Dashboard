Privacy Policy for F1 Command Center

Effective Date: June 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the F1 Command Center mobile and web application ("the Application") handles information. We believe in absolute privacy, and our application is designed from the ground up to respect your data.

1. Zero Data Collection

F1 Command Center is a client-side dashboard tool. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage metrics. There are no external databases or analytics trackers integrated into this application.

2. Local Storage of Settings

Any configuration you perform inside the application—including clock timezones, your Todo list tasks, completed study sessions, streaks, and calendar events—is saved locally on your own device using standard web storage (localStorage). This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

3. Third-Party Web Services

To provide real-time information, the Application makes direct network requests from your device to the following third-party services:

  • Open-Meteo API: Used to fetch local weather telemetry. Your location coordinates are sent to this service to retrieve weather data but are never stored or logged by us.
  • Wikimedia Commons: Used to load public domain driver images in the F1 memorabilia widget.
  • Google News & BBC News RSS Feeds: Queried locally to cross-reference and present the latest world and tech headlines.

These external services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their terms if you have questions about how they process network requests.

4. Security

Because all data remains local to your device, the security of your information is safeguarded by the built-in operating system security of your mobile phone or browser.

5. Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any personal data, this Application is completely safe for children of all ages and complies with COPPA and GDPR requirements.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at: support@abhi.personal.dashboard.
